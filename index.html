✏️ How to edit: Click any question to expand it. To add a new FAQ, copy one <div class="faq-item"> block and update the data-q attribute and inner text. The search uses data-q as the question index.
How do I log in to my Quipper account? ▼
Go to quipper.com/ph and click "Log In." Enter your registered email and password. If you haven't received credentials, contact your school administrator or CSO.
How do I reset my password? ▼
On the login page, click "Forgot Password." Enter your registered email — a reset link will be sent to your inbox. Check spam if it doesn't arrive within 5 minutes.
How do I create a class in QLink? ▼
Log in to QLink and click "+ Create Class." Fill in the class name, subject, grade level, and school year. Once saved, a class code is generated — share it with students to enroll.
Why can't my students access my assignments? ▼
Check: (1) Assignment is published, not a draft. (2) Due date and open date are correct. (3) Students are enrolled using your class code. (4) No restricted access schedule is blocking them.
How do I view student progress and scores? ▼
In QLink, open your class and click the "Progress" tab. You'll see completion rates, scores, and time-on-task per student per activity. Reports can also be exported.
How do I assign activities to students? ▼
Inside your class in QLink, click "+ Assign." Choose from the Quipper content library or your QCreate materials. Set the schedule and due date, then click "Publish."
How do I review and score essay submissions? ▼
Go to QEssay and open the assignment. Click a student's name to read their essay. Add written feedback and assign a score, then click "Save" — the student will be notified.

1
Create your Quipper teacher account
Visit quipper.com/ph and click "Sign Up as Teacher." Use your official school email. Provide your full name, school name, and subject. Verify your email to activate the account.
2
Update your profile information
Click your name at the top-right → "My Profile." Fill in your full name, subject area, grade level, and upload a profile photo. Click "Save Changes."
3
Set a secure password
Go to Settings → Security → Change Password. Use at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
4
Link your account to your school
Under Settings → School Information, search for your school by name or DepEd ID. Select and confirm to link to your institution's subscription.
5
Create your first class in QLink
Go to QLink → "+ Create Class." Enter the class details. A class code is generated — share it with students so they can enroll.
✏️ How to edit: Add new troubleshooting entries by copying any <div class="faq-item"> block and updating the data-q attribute and content.
I cannot log in — "Invalid email or password" ▼
Step 1: Check for typos in your email.
Step 2: Click "Forgot Password" to reset via email.
Step 3: Clear browser cache (Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data).
Step 4: Try Google Chrome or an incognito window. If still failing, contact your CSO.
The website loads slowly or won't open ▼
Step 1: Check your internet connection.
Step 2: Try an Incognito/Private window.
Step 3: Disable browser extensions.
Step 4: Update your browser. Google Chrome is recommended.
A student can't find or access my class ▼
Step 1: Confirm the student is using the exact class code from QLink → Class Settings.
Step 2: Check the class isn't archived.
Step 3: Make sure the student is logged in to the correct account.
Step 4: Contact your administrator if the class is completely missing.
Assignment is published but students can't see it ▼
Step 1: Confirm status shows "Published" — not Draft.
Step 2: Check the open date is not set to a future date.
Step 3: Verify students are enrolled in this class.
Step 4: Ask students to refresh or log out and back in.
Videos won't play or keep buffering ▼
Step 1: Check internet speed — video requires a stable connection.
Step 2: Lower video quality in the player settings.
Step 3: Clear cache and disable extensions.
Step 4: Use Google Chrome on desktop for best performance.

Quick Guides
✏️ How to edit: Update questions and answers below. Add a data-q attribute matching the question text for search indexing.
How do I log in to my student account? ▼
Go to quipper.com/ph and click "Log In." Enter the email and password your teacher gave you. If you forgot, ask your teacher — students cannot reset passwords on their own.
How do I join my teacher's class? ▼
After logging in, click "Join a Class" on your dashboard. Enter the class code your teacher gave you. The class will appear on your dashboard right away.
How do I answer an assignment or quiz? ▼
Open your class, then click the assignment from the list. Answer each question carefully, then click "Submit." Do not close the browser before submitting or your answers may not be saved.
How do I know if my work was submitted? ▼
A "Submitted" confirmation screen appears after you click Submit. You can also check under "Completed Activities" for your score or a "Submitted" status.
How do I submit an essay assignment? ▼
Open the essay assignment, type your response in the text box, review it, then click "Submit." Your teacher will read and provide feedback and a score.
How do I check my score on a completed activity? ▼
Click the "Completed" tab in your class. Find and click the activity to see your score and correct answers. For essays, wait for your teacher to grade and release the score.
I have overdue activities — can I still submit? ▼
Activities marked "Overdue" are past their due date. Whether you can still submit depends on your teacher's settings. Contact your teacher directly to ask for an extension.
